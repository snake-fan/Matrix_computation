\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & a

\subsection*{2)}

\textbf{Solution.}

First, we find the eigenvalues by solving the characteristic equation $|\mathbf{A} - \lambda \mathbf{I}| = 0$:

$$\det
\begin{bmatrix} 2-\lambda & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2-\lambda & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & a-\lambda
\end{bmatrix} = 0$$

It is obviously that:
$$
(1-\lambda)(\lambda^2 - (3+a)\lambda + 3a - 2) = 0
$$

Thus we can get that $\lambda_1 = 1$
and $\lambda_2 + \lambda_3 = 3a-2$, $\lambda_2 \cdot \lambda_3 = (3+a) / 2$

Since $\A$ is PSD, we need to ensure:
$$
\lambda_2 + \lambda_3 \ge 0 \quad and \quad \lambda_2 \cdot \lambda_3 \ge 0
$$

Thus we can get:
$$
a \ge \frac{2}{3}
$$
